ADR 0077: Decision records are never translated

Accepted

{{ t.html('admin.docs.source', file='adr/0077-decision-records-are-never-translated.md') }} {{ t('admin.decisions.back') }}

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-09-24
  • Extends: ADR 0020, ADR 0058, ADR 0062, ADR 0063

Context

The public guides have German, French and Spanish sources under docs/de/, docs/fr/ and docs/es/ (ADR 0058, ADR 0062, ADR 0063), and every visible string of the web application exists in all four catalogues under webapp/locales/. The operator area also renders every record in adr/ under /admin/decisions, generated by scripts/build_frontend_documentation.py into frontend/templates/admin-decisions/ and webapp/decision_records.py. With translated chrome around it, a record looks like one more page waiting for its German, French and Spanish counterparts.

A decision record is not documentation of how to use the service. It is the repository's own history: the exact wording a decision was accepted with, which later records cite, supersede and quote. Accepted records are never rewritten, so a translation could never be corrected against its source by editing the record either. A second text in another language is a second version of the decision that can disagree with the first, and nobody could say which one was accepted.

Decision

A record in adr/ exists in English only, and only once. There is no adr/de/, adr/fr/, adr/es/ or any other translated copy, whether written by hand, generated at build time or produced by a translation service at runtime.

The operator area shows the English record in every locale. /admin/decisions and /admin/decisions/<slug> render the one generated template for each record regardless of the request's language. The surrounding interface - navigation, headings, filters, the notice that a record is shown as it was written - follows the request's locale through webapp/locales/ like the rest of the operator area (ADR 0020).

Titles and statuses are not translated either. The index row in adr/README.md is the one source of both; webapp/decision_records.py and the operator search index carry them verbatim in every language overlay.

Translation work never touches adr/. TRANSLATING.md and the translation tooling treat the directory as out of scope; a change that adds a translated record, or a locale key that restates a record's title or text, is refused in review.

Consequences

Every citation of "ADR NNNN" points at exactly one text, and superseding a record means changing one Status line, not four. Writing a record costs no translation review, and the frontend builder needs no per-locale decision templates.

Operators who do not read English read the records in English or with their own tools. That is accepted: the records explain the project's reasoning to its contributors, and the guides that tell an operator what to do remain translated under ADR 0058, ADR 0062 and ADR 0063.

Alternatives considered

Translated records alongside the English ones, like the guides. Rejected: a guide describes current behaviour and is revised with it, while a record is frozen once accepted, so a translation error could never be fixed without editing an accepted record, and two texts could claim the same decision.

Machine translation when a record is displayed. Rejected for the same reason as for the guides in ADR 0058: it adds a network or model dependency and makes the text vary between visits - for a record, that means the decision itself would appear to change.

Translated titles only. Rejected: the title is the decision stated as a sentence, and translating it already produces the second, possibly disagreeing, wording this record rules out.
