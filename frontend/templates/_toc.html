Every label is a section's own heading string rather than a second wording of it, so a heading rewritten in one language cannot leave its entry here saying the old thing in the other three. A page with fewer than two sections includes nothing: a contents list of one entry is a link to the top of the page the reader is already on. An entry may carry a `group` label. When any does, the entries render as labelled columns in the order their groups first appear, so a long list (the scan report has up to twelve) reads as a few short ones instead of a wall of pills. #} {% if toc | length > 1 %}
